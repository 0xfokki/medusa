The last hour, off the chain

Every token with real wallets trading it in the last 60 minutes, read from Transfer events against the Uniswap pools, not from an aggregator. Routers and contracts are dropped; a wallet that bought and sold the same size is not a trade. Deepest liquidity first.

GET/api/v1/verdicts

Words vs money, token by token

The chain's lean against the crowd's lean, one row per token with at least 3 wallets trading. CONFIRMED when both point the same way, CONTRADICTED when they don't, CHAIN ONLY when there aren't two tagged posts to read. RUNNER WATCH is where real buyers outnumber sellers 3-to-1 with nothing on social against it. Not a price call.

How she scores

The chain's lean is (buyers - sellers) / wallets over the last hour. The crowd's lean is (bull - bear) / tagged posts for today. Where both exist they are averaged and scaled to ±100; where the crowd is silent the chain speaks alone and the row says so. A runner needs a chain lean of at least 0.5 across at least 5 wallets, with the crowd not against it.

score = ((buyers - sellers) / wallets + (bull - bear) / posts) / 2 × 100

Every figure here is also on the page, and every fill links to its transaction. If a number looks wrong, the transaction is the place to check.
